ACME CORPORATION — INCIDENT RESPONSE PROCEDURES
Document ID: SEC-IRP-2025-001 | Effective: January 1, 2025

REPORTING
Security incidents must be reported to security@acme-corp.com within 1 hour.
Critical incidents must also be reported to the SOC hotline at ext. 9911.
Employees must not attempt unauthorized remediation of suspected breaches.

SEVERITY LEVELS
CRITICAL: data exfiltration, malware outbreak, active intrusion.
HIGH: privilege escalation, policy violation with data exposure.
WARNING: failed login clusters, suspicious outbound traffic.

RESPONSE TIMELINE
CRITICAL incidents: containment within 15 minutes, executive notification within 30 minutes.
All incidents receive a ticket ID in format SEC-YYYY-NNNN.

POST-INCIDENT
Root cause analysis required within 5 business days for CRITICAL and HIGH events.

CONTACT: security@acme-corp.com | SOC Hotline: ext. 9911
