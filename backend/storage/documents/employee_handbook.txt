Employee handbook code of conduct requires integrity and ethical behavior from all employees.

WELCOME
Welcome to ACME Corporation. This handbook outlines workplace standards, employee rights,
and company expectations for all full-time and part-time staff.

CODE OF CONDUCT
The employee handbook code of conduct requires integrity and ethical behavior from all staff.
All employees must act with integrity, respect colleagues, and comply with applicable laws.
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are strictly prohibited.
Conflicts of interest must be reported to HR within 5 business days.

WORKPLACE EXPECTATIONS
Employees are expected to maintain professional conduct in all internal and client interactions.
Attendance at mandatory training sessions is required.
Dress code: business casual unless client-facing meetings require business formal.

REPORTING CONCERNS
Employees may report ethics concerns anonymously via ethics@acme-corp.com.
HR will acknowledge reports within 2 business days.
