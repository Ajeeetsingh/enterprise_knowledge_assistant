ACME CORPORATION — REMOTE WORK POLICY
Document ID: HR-RWP-2025-001 | Effective: January 1, 2025

HYBRID WORK
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with written manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and annual review.

CORE HOURS
Core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local timezone.
Employees must be reachable via Slack, email, and video conferencing during core hours.

EQUIPMENT AND SECURITY
Company-issued laptops must use ACME endpoint protection and full-disk encryption.
VPN is mandatory when accessing internal systems from outside the office.
Remote workers in Finance, HR, and Engineering must enroll in MFA by June 30, 2025.

INTERNATIONAL REMOTE WORK
Limited to 30 calendar days per year with prior HR and Legal approval.
