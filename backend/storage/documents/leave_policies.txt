ACME CORPORATION — LEAVE POLICIES
Document ID: HR-LVE-2025-001 | Effective: January 1, 2025

SICK LEAVE POLICY
How many sick days do employees receive? Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave annually.

ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year (1.67 days per month).
Leave requests require 14 days advance notice via Workday.

SICK LEAVE
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave annually.
Sick leave allowance: 10 days per year for all full-time employees.
A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

MATERNITY LEAVE
Primary caregivers: 16 weeks fully paid parental leave.
Maternity leave provides 16 weeks of fully paid leave for primary caregivers.
Leave must begin within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.
Employees must notify HR at least 30 days before intended start date.

PATERNITY AND SECONDARY CAREGIVER LEAVE
Secondary caregivers: 4 weeks fully paid parental leave.

BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
Immediate family: 5 paid days. Extended family: 3 paid days.

CONTACT: hr@acme-corp.com | Leave portal: Workday
